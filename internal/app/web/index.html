About the title data

The first download from IMDb can be a few hundred MB and take several minutes. Smart Search then builds a compact local index.

All sizes download the same source files. Larger options retain more titles and use more memory. Changing size takes effect after a successful rebuild; your current catalogue stays available while it runs.

Alternative titles add IMDb's regional title list to the download and keep only titles in your chosen language for movies and series already in your catalogue. English, original and translated names all keep working.

IMDb data is for personal, non-commercial use. Data terms ↗

Using Stremio or Stremio Enhanced?

These apps have no built-in reorder control. Two options:

Addon manager: open the community Stremio Addon Manager, sign in, choose Load addons, drag Stremio Smart Search to the top, then Sync to Stremio.

This is a third-party tool. It is not affiliated with Stremio Smart Search or Stremio, and it asks for your Stremio login. Use it at your own discretion.

No sign-in: newly installed addons go to the bottom. Remove your other addons and reinstall them after Stremio Smart Search. Note their install links first.

What about my other addons?

Move only Stremio Smart Search. The others keep their relative order, including your stream providers: this addon supplies search rows only. Your account's addon order may sync to other devices. Reorder controls vary between clients.

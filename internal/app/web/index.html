How search works

Titles are matched in memory on this computer, so there is no remote search server to wait for. Posters and playback still use your online providers.

No AI service, account passwords or watch history are used. This local link works on this computer; a TV or phone needs its own reachable helper.

Install through Stremio Web ↗

Use the same Stremio account to sync the addon to your desktop app. Your browser may ask permission to connect to this computer.

03

Move Smart Search to the top

Recommended, so its search results appear first. In dx.bi: Addons → Order & backup → move to top → Save order.

What about my other addons?

Move only Stremio Smart Search. The others keep their relative order, including your stream providers: this addon supplies search rows only. Your account's addon order may sync to other devices. Reorder controls vary between clients.
